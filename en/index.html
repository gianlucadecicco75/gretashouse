Holiday home in Otranto, Selva del Turchese: a comfortable apartment close to the sea, ideal for families and couples. Book your stay in Salento.

Discover Salento with us!

Welcome to Greta's House, the perfect place for a relaxing holiday immersed in the nature of Salento. Located in the Selva del Turchese complex, 3 km from Otranto, we offer a fully equipped countryside apartment for your stay.

A bright 70 m² apartment, ideal for 2 to 4 people, with private access and a veranda with outdoor shower, featuring air conditioning, TV, a double bedroom and a small room with bunk beds, a bathroom with shower, a fully equipped kitchenette including a coffee machine, washing machine, gas detector, fire extinguisher and a private parking space.

Far from the crowds so you can fully enjoy your relaxation, just 3 km from Otranto — about a 3-minute drive.

A 1.5 km path along the Idro river, walkable or by bike, leads to the centre of Otranto, keeping you immersed in nature and the wonderful colours and scents of our Salento.

Selva del Turchese is set in the green Mediterranean scrub of the Idro Valley, a peaceful area with green spaces and the complex's private pine forest.

Not far away you'll find the Palascìa Lighthouse, the easternmost point of Italy, now famous for the "Alba dei Popoli" (Dawn of the Peoples), one of the most spectacular natural sunrises in the world.

Just a few km away you'll find many seaside destinations: Porto Badisco, Santa Cesarea Terme, Castro, Baia dei Turchi, Alimini Lakes, Torre dell'Orso, Lecce.

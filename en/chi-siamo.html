About us

Holiday home in Salento, in the Selva del Turchese complex

Welcome to Greta's House, the perfect place for a relaxing holiday in the beautiful Salento countryside. We offer a cozy, fully equipped apartment surrounded by a peaceful, natural atmosphere. Book your stay with us and enjoy the beauty and authenticity of Salento.

  • Private apartment for couples and families
  • Garden with barbecue
  • Relaxation surrounded by the scents of Salento

The apartment

A bright 70 m² apartment, ideal for 2 to 4 people, with private access and a veranda with outdoor shower, nestled in the Salento countryside and surrounded by woods and pine forests, within the Selva del Turchese complex. It features air conditioning, TV, a double bedroom and a small room with bunk beds, a bathroom with shower, a fully equipped kitchenette including a coffee machine, washing machine, gas detector and fire extinguisher, and a private parking space.

Far from the crowds so you can fully enjoy your relaxation, just 3 km from Otranto — about a 3-minute drive. A 1.5 km path along the Idro river, walkable or by bike, leads to the centre of Otranto.

Selva del Turchese is set in the green Mediterranean scrub of the Idro Valley, a peaceful area with green spaces and the complex's private pine forest.

The area

Not far away you'll find the Palascìa Lighthouse, the easternmost point of Italy, now famous for the "Alba dei Popoli" (Dawn of the Peoples), one of the most spectacular natural sunrises in the world.

Just a few km away you'll find many seaside destinations: Porto Badisco, Santa Cesarea Terme, Castro, Baia dei Turchi, Alimini Lakes, Torre dell'Orso, Lecce.
